Q&A

Before you start

About the checker, your data and the expert review

Does this score show whether ChatGPT mentions my brand?

No. The six signals assess how ready your website is for AI search. Actual brand mentions and citations need a separate audit

Why do you ask for my work email?

The live service will use your work email to deliver the report and arrange any review you request. This prototype only previews the email and sends nothing

Is it really free?

The website check and 30-minute expert review are free. A deeper audit, implementation and monitoring are paid services, with scope and pricing discussed separately

What data do you store?

This prototype keeps your website, email, consent and review details only in the current browser tab. Refreshing clears the session. The footer links to Square Group’s Personal Data Protection Policy

What happens in the 30-minute review?

We review the findings, agree on priorities and discuss whether a deeper audit would help. Requesting a review does not commit you to a paid service
