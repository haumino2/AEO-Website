# Typography and light-palette refinement

Restored Funnel Display for English headings and Google Sans for body text. Vietnamese headings use Google Sans throughout to avoid mixing Vietnamese fallback glyphs into Funnel Display. Fonts use local variable WOFF2 subsets with font-display: swap; OFL licenses are included beside the assets.

Heading weight is reduced to 550, body remains 400 and CTAs use 600. The desktop headline is balanced into two lines, form labels are smaller, and operator/method copy is lower in the hierarchy.

White, navy #13203F and cyan #00AEEF are retained. Forms/cards use a pale blue-gray surface, more subtle borders and a restrained cyan accent. The number strip uses lighter dividers, larger numerals and smaller labels.

No content, scoring, lead fields, API behavior or language defaults changed. Verified local EN/VI font loading, desktop layouts, report/review flow and 375px mobile overflow. At 375 × 812 the primary form CTA remains within the viewport.

The previous PageSpeed 100 run predates this font restoration; it is historical evidence, not a new performance score for this refinement.
